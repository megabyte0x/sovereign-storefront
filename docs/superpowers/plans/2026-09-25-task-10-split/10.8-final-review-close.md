## Fix loop

Then the orchestrator re-runs the Verify blocks that the change touches, and a fresh reviewer re-reviews only the fix diff. At most 3 rounds, then stop and ask the user. A minor is fixed if it is cheap and local; otherwise it is written into the ledger as accepted.
